# 08 — Report

Purpose: produce the formal RFSU deliverable.

## Constraints

- Accessible Swedish
- Maximum 30 pages
- Evidence-based and traceable
- Useful as:
  - knowledge base;
  - influence/advocacy support;
  - learning between participating clinics;
  - concrete improvement support.

## Working structure

1. Executive summary
2. Assignment and method
3. What the three cases show
4. Cross-case findings
5. Structural conditions
6. Good practice and mechanisms
7. Barriers
8. Transferability
9. Recommendations
10. Method/limitations
11. References

Case material may be presented compactly, but the report's main narrative should be thematic and comparative rather than three isolated clinic chapters.
